# Marketing Plan

## Status
In progress — strategy and channels TBD.

## Target Audiences
- People with low vision, macular degeneration, cone-rod dystrophy, other visual impairments
- Older adults with age-related vision loss
- Caregivers and family members of low-vision users

## Channels to Explore
- Paid search (Google Ads — keywords: magnifier app, low vision app, video magnifier)
- Social media (Facebook/Instagram — older demographic skews well)
- Low vision / blindness community forums and subreddits
- Partnerships with optometrists, ophthalmologists, vision rehab therapists
- Accessibility-focused blogs and YouTube channels

## Messaging
- No ads, no subscriptions, no data collection
- On-device OCR + TTS — works offline
- Designed for real low-vision use cases (not just a camera zoom)
- Free web version to try before buying the app

## Assets Needed
- Ad creative (static + video)
- App Store screenshots optimized for search
- Press kit / one-pager for outreach
