# Physical Product: Hat Clip Mount

## Concept
A physical accessory that holds the phone hands-free during extended reading sessions. Designed for low-vision users who need to read for long periods — menus, documents, etc.

## Use Cases
- Reading documents without holding the phone
- Hands-free magnification while eating (e.g., reading a menu)
- Extended reading sessions (mail, newspapers, books)

## Options to Explore

### Hat Clip Mount
- Clips to cap brim
- Phone faces downward for reading-view
- Target user: someone reading something flat on a table

### Table Stand
- Small foldable stand that props the phone at reading angle on a flat surface
- More stable, less portable than hat clip
